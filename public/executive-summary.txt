Asset Value of C-Band Spectrum Based on FCC Auction 107

The strategic importance of QuSat’s C-Band spectrum is underscored by FCC Auction 107 (2020–2021), in which 280 MHz of the lower C-Band (3.7–3.98 GHz) was reallocated for terrestrial 5G and auctioned for over $80 billion. Major U.S. telecom operators including Verizon, AT&T, and T-Mobile acquired these licenses, with Verizon alone committing approximately $45.5 billion.

This reallocation materially reduced the amount of C-Band spectrum available for satellite services, increasing the scarcity and value of remaining FCC-authorized C-Band spectrum allocated to Fixed Satellite Service (FSS). QuSat’s licensed C-Band spectrum, which remains fully protected for satellite use, therefore represents a high-value infrastructure asset positioned for long-term monetization.
